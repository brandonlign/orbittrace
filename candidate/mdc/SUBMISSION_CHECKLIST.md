# Submission checklist

This checklist separates finished preparation from the work that still needs outside input.

## Prepared

- [x] 95-row GMN lookup table for the five significant years.
- [x] Arithmetic-mean JSON record and legacy-text record.
- [x] Calculation record for the submitted mean values.
- [x] Official MDC radiant/orbit consistency check with no flagged errors.
- [x] Current official-catalogue comparison with no matching solution under the fixed rules.
- [x] Exact rerun of the primary 101-event selection and 95-event confirmed table.
- [x] Blind January–July 2026 rediscovery.
- [x] Internal source, bootstrap, geographic, and sensitivity reruns.
- [x] Reproduction of the CAMS and SonotaCo supporting samples.
- [x] Working manuscript and reviewer guide.

## Still required

- [ ] Independent review by a meteor-shower specialist.
- [ ] Independent check for obscure historical, removed, or differently parameterized shower solutions.
- [ ] Review of the antihelion background and the non-orbital activity selection.
- [ ] Review of the distinction between the robust matching solution and arithmetic-mean MDC record.
- [ ] Confirmation of GMN data-use, acknowledgment, and authorship expectations.
- [ ] Final author, affiliation, contact, and journal information.
- [ ] Revision of the manuscript in response to expert comments.
- [ ] Regeneration of the final submission package and hashes after those revisions.

## Fields left blank

The MDC or publication process supplies these fields:

- official numerical shower code;
- three-letter code;
- provisional designation;
- official shower name; and
- final publication reference.
