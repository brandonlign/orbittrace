## Interpretation

The result shows that the signal is not generated by one country, regional camera network, station cluster, or observing night. Three disjoint geographic station groups independently contain a significant activity enhancement and a compact, mutually consistent orbit.

This remains a robustness test inside the common GMN processing system. It is not equivalent to an independently reduced external network, which remains a publication-stage goal.
