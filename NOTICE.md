The OrbitTrace source code is licensed under the MIT License in `LICENSE`.

The repository also contains small derived tables from external meteor catalogues. Those rows are not relicensed as MIT software:

- GMN level-3 trajectory data are released by the Global Meteor Network under CC BY 4.0.
- CAMS and SonotaCo data are distributed publicly through the IAU Meteor Data Center with source/reference guidance.
- EDMOND states that its database may be downloaded and used freely with the requested citations.
- IAU MDC shower/catalogue information should be cited to the database and the underlying references.

Source URLs, coverage, and file-level provenance are recorded in `data/README.md`.

For a Zenodo archive of the full repository, treat this as a mixed-license upload rather than applying the MIT License to every file. Zenodo supports multiple licenses for mixed uploads; MIT applies to the code and CC BY 4.0 applies to GMN-derived data. Other derived excerpts retain their source attribution and reuse conditions.
