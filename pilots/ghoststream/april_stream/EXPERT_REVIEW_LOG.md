## Decision rules

- A reviewer response is evidence to evaluate, not automatic authority.
- A specific reproducible error overrides prior confidence.
- A preference without evidence does not automatically change the frozen analysis.
- No threshold may be relaxed after review to preserve a positive result.
- Any new analysis prompted by criticism must be labeled exploratory or separately preregistered.
- A credible duplicate shower or coordinate-convention error pauses MDC outreach immediately.
- Conflicting expert opinions must be preserved and adjudicated explicitly.
- Silence is not approval.
- Do not quote or name a reviewer without permission.
