# Blind rediscovery

The January–July 2026 search was rerun from the recovered all-season source. The runner received the year and month but not the final April center or the 95-event lookup table.

A small wrapper repair was needed because the original report code used fixed historical year labels. The repair changed only output keys and labels; it did not change candidate generation, scoring, thresholds, known-shower comparison, or validation logic. The original and repaired source hashes are recorded in `../blind_wrapper_fix.json`.

The April survivor contained 26 discovery members. Its regenerated center matched the saved discovery center to 2.86 × 10⁻¹⁵ in the verifier’s normalized coordinates.

The recovered search then validated the candidate in 2025 and 2024, yielding 36 and 14 members respectively, with p = 0.002 in each validation year. The measurement-uncertainty check passed in all 500 discovery-stage clone trials.

No non-April month produced another full-gate survivor.

This establishes the discovery lineage: the April structure can be recovered by the original blind search without supplying its final month or member list. It does not prove that the structure is absent from all historical shower literature; that is a separate expert-review question.
